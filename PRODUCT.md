# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Uni students and young professionals near Melbourne CBD (primarily RMIT area). On-the-go, decision-fatigued, wanting a quick confident answer to "where should I actually go right now?" — study spots, cheap food, afternoon coffee, something to do. Casual, often mobile, with an immediate specific need.

## Product Purpose
An AI-powered local discovery tool: users describe what they want in plain English and receive curated real-place recommendations nearby. Dual purpose — a genuinely useful tool for the Melbourne student community, and a portfolio showcase for Sean Tay demonstrating Claude API integration and thoughtful product craft.

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, approachable. The feeling of a recommendation from a knowledgeable friend who knows all the good spots — not a search engine, not an AI assistant. Confident without being slick. Enthusiastic without being loud.

## Anti-references
- AI chatbot aesthetic (chat bubbles, typing indicators, ChatGPT/Perplexity feel)
- Neon / cyberpunk dark mode (high-saturation neons, gaming aesthetics)
- Yelp / TripAdvisor (heavy review grids, star ratings everywhere, directory clutter)
- Generic SaaS (white + blue-accent, Notion/Webflow clone feel)

## Design Principles
1. Friend energy, not assistant energy — recommendations should feel personal, not algorithmic
2. Results front and center — the places are the product; everything else is scaffolding
3. Light enough to be approachable, sharp enough to impress — portfolio context demands craft without coldness
4. Confident and decisive — no hedging language, no excessive UI states

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. Keyboard navigable, sufficient contrast on muted text against dark backgrounds, reduced-motion support required.
